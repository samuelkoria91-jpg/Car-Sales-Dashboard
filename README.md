 ## Introduction
 When managing multi-country *sales operations*, tracking macro metrics alongside granular regional demand is key to driving growth. This dashboard transforms raw sales transactions into a strategic narrative letting stakeholders quickly evaluate high-level performance and drill directly into regional market dynamics

## What This Dashboard is All About
This dashboard provides an end-to-end overview of vehicle sales performance, profitability, and distribution across distinct geographic segments:

**Macro Financial Health**: Displays critical business KPIs at a glance, highlighting 9M in Revenue, 1M in Profit, and 347 total units sold.

**Geographic Performance**: Maps out market shares clearly, revealing that South Africa drove the majority of revenue at *6M (67.42%)*, compared to Botswana at 3M (32.58%).

**Product Demand**: Breaks down the top-performing vehicle models, showcasing strong consumer preferences for vehicles like the 3 Series and Qashqai.

**Segment Insights**: Uses a dual-axis visual to map Quantity vs. Profit by vehicle segment, proving that Sedans yielded the highest profitability (accounting for 32.14% of total profit).

## Dashboard Overview

![Car Dashboard](/Car%20Sales%20Bashboard.PNG)
**🌍 Regional Market Dominance**

The automotive sales market across the analyzed region is heavily driven by South Africa, which serves as the primary revenue engine.

Revenue Distribution: South Africa accounts for the lion's share of the market, generating 6M (67.42%) of total revenue.

Secondary Market: Botswana represents a strong secondary market, contributing 3M (32.58%) to the overall revenue mix.

Geographic Density: High-density sales clusters are concentrated around key metropolitan areas in both nations, as highlighted by the geospatial mapping.

**📊 Financial Health & Volume**

The operations maintain a healthy profit margin relative to overall sales volume.

Top-Line Revenue: Total revenue reached 9M against a total sales volume of 347 units.

Bottom-Line Profit: Total profitability stands at 1M, showcasing a solid overall return on sales across the 15 models tracked.

**🚗 Segment Performance & Profit Drivers**

Consumer demand and profit margins vary significantly by vehicle type, with certain segments yielding much higher returns despite unit volumes.

The Profit Engine (Sedans): Sedans are the undisputed market leader in financial performance. They generated the highest profit, accounting for 32.14% of total profits ($359,600).

The Margin Divergence (Hatchbacks): Hatchbacks show a notable divergence between volume and returns. While unit volume remains relatively high (103 units sold), it resulted in the lowest profit segment at $153,000—indicating tighter margins or a lower average selling price for this vehicle type.

Steady Contenders (SUVs & Pickups): SUVs (71 units) and Pickups (48 units) maintain mid-tier volume positioning but provide stable revenue support to the product mix.
